## 题目

The chart below shows numbers of incidents and injuries per 100 million passenger miles travelled (PMT) by transportation type in 2002.

## 原范文

The bar chart compares the number of incidents and injuries for every 100 million passenger miles travelled on five different types of public transport in 2002.

It is clear that the most incidents and injuries took place on demand-response vehicles. By contrast, commuter rail services recorded by far the lowest figures.

A total of 225 incidents and 173 injuries, per 100 million passenger miles travelled, took place on demand-response transport services. These figures were nearly three times as high as those for the second highest category, bus services. There were 76 incidents and 66 people were injured on buses.

Rail services experienced fewer problems. The number of incidents on light rail trains equaled the figure recorded for buses, but there were significantly fewer injuries, at only 39. Heavy rail services saw lower numbers of such events than light rail services, but commuter rail passengers were even less likely to experience problems. In fact, only 20 incidents and 17 injuries occurred on commuter trains.

## 参考修改版(Claude)

The bar chart compares incident and injury rates on five types of public transport in 2002, measured per 100 million passenger miles travelled.

It is clear that demand-response vehicles had by far the highest rates of incidents and injuries, while commuter rail recorded the lowest figures of all. It is also noticeable that all three types of rail service had fewer injuries than buses.

Demand-response services recorded 225 incidents and 173 injuries. The incident figure was nearly three times that of buses, the second highest category, which saw 76 incidents and 66 injuries.

Rail services recorded lower figures. Light rail had the same number of incidents as buses (76), but only 39 injuries. Heavy rail figures were lower than those for light rail [补:对照原图写出重轨的具体数值], and commuter rail was lower still, with just 20 incidents and 17 injuries.

### 修改说明

| 位置 | 原文 | 修改 | 原因 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 开头句 | compares the number of incidents and injuries for every 100 million passenger miles travelled on five different types of public transport(33 词) | compares incident and injury rates on five types of public transport …, measured per 100 million passenger miles travelled(约 24 词) | 用 rates 代替 the number of;去掉 different;单位后置,介词短语不再堆叠 |
| 概述 | 只有最高 / 最低 | 增加「三种铁路的伤亡人数都少于公交」 | 概述应给出整体规律,而不只是极值 |
| 倍数 | These figures were nearly three times as high … | 改为只对事故数用「近三倍」 | 225÷76≈2.96,但 173÷66≈2.6,对伤亡数说近三倍偏松 |
| 重复单位 | 主体段再次写 per 100 million passenger miles travelled | 主体段删去 | 单位已在开头交代,重复多余 |
| 用词 | experienced fewer problems | recorded lower figures | problems 太虚,与数据对应的是 figures |
| 重轨 | lower numbers of such events(无数据) | 加占位提示,请对照原图补数 | 三组中只有重轨没有数据,主体段不均衡 |
