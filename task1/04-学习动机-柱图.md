## 题目

The charts below show the main reasons for study among students of different groups and the amount of support they received from employers.

## 原范文

The bar charts compare students of different ages in terms of why they are studying and whether they are supported by an employer.

It is clear that the proportion of students who study for career purposes is far higher among the younger age groups, while the oldest students are more likely to study for interest. Employer support is more commonly given to younger students.

Around 80% of students aged under 26 study to further their careers, whereas only 10% study purely out of interest. The gap between these two proportions narrows as students get older, and the figures for those in their forties are the same, at about 40%. Students aged over 49 overwhelmingly study for interest (70%) rather than for professional reasons (less than 20%).

Just over 60% of students aged under 26 are supported by their employers. By contrast, the 30-39 age group is the most self-sufficient, with only 30% being given time off and help with fees. The figures rise slightly for students in their forties and for those aged 50 or more.

## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 双图题怎么写**
两张图、同一个主题(按年龄组分类的学生):
- 开头一句话概括两张图。
- 概述同时概括两张图的要点(第一张:学习原因;第二张:雇主支持)。
- 主体**一张图一段**:第 3 段写学习原因,第 4 段写雇主支持。

**2. 按年龄组分组**
最年轻(under 26)、中间(30–39、40 多岁)、最年长(50 以上)三层对比,并点出变化趋势:`The gap … narrows as students get older`,以及两项数据相等的交点:`the figures for those in their forties are the same, at about 40%`。

**3. 时态:这篇用一般现在时**
题目没有给年份,是一份调查的现状,所以用现在时(study、are supported、rise)。对比前几篇:

| 篇目 | 数据特点 | 时态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 交通事故 / 02 各地房价 | 有具体年份 | 过去时 |
| 03 奥运奖牌 | 累计到现在的总数 | 现在完成时 + 现在时 |
| 04 学习动机 | 没有年份的调查数据 | 一般现在时 |

**4. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| compare students … in terms of why they are studying and whether they are supported by an employer | in terms of 后面接 why / whether 引导的从句,概括图表主题 |
| Around 80% … study to further their careers, whereas only 10% study purely out of interest | whereas 对比同一组人的两个数据 |
| The gap between these two proportions narrows as students get older | 描述趋势:gap + narrows + as … |
| the figures for those in their forties are the same, at about 40% | those 代替 students,at about 40% 带出数字 |
| overwhelmingly study for interest (70%) rather than for professional reasons (less than 20%) | rather than 对比,前后结构平行 |
| the 30-39 age group is …, with only 30% being given time off and help with fees | with + 数字补充细节 |

**5. 描述年龄的说法**
`aged under 26`、`aged 50 or over`、`in their forties`、`the 30-39 age group`、`the oldest students`。

**6. 平行结构(比较的规则的延伸)**
`study for interest rather than for professional reasons`:rather than 前后都是 `for + 名词`,结构一致。前面练的比较规则是「两边要同类」,这里是同样的道理。
