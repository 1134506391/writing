## 题目

The chart below shows information about changes in average house prices in five different cities between 1990 and 2002 compared with the average house prices in 1989.

## 原范文

The bar chart compares the cost of an average house in five major cities over a period of 13 years from 1989.

We can see that house prices fell overall between 1990 and 1995, but most of the cities saw rising prices between 1996 and 2002. London experienced by far the greatest changes in house prices over the 13-year period.

Over the 5 years after 1989, the cost of average homes in Tokyo and London dropped by around 7%, while New York house prices went down by 5%. By contrast, prices rose by approximately 2% in both Madrid and Frankfurt.

Between 1996 and 2002, London house prices jumped to around 12% above the 1989 average. Homebuyers in New York also had to pay significantly more, with prices rising to 5% above the 1989 average, but homes in Tokyo remained cheaper than they were in 1989. The cost of an average home in Madrid rose by a further 2%, while prices in Frankfurt remained stable.

## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 基准年柱图怎么读**
纵轴是相对 1989 年的百分比变化,1989 年本身是 0%。0 线以上是高于 1989 年,以下是低于。所以要写 `X% above / below the 1989 level`,不是写绝对价格。

**2. 怎么分组**
五个城市 × 两个时间段,不要按城市逐个写。范文的做法是**先按时间段分段,段内再按趋势方向分组**:第一段 Tokyo 和 London 一起(都下降约 7%),New York 单独,Madrid 和 Frankfurt 一起(都上升约 2%)。

**3. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| X dropped by around 7%, while Y went down by 5%. | while 连接两个相近的数据 |
| By contrast, prices rose by approximately 2% in both Madrid and Frankfurt. | 用 by contrast 转向相反的趋势;both A and B 合并两个城市 |
| London house prices jumped to around 12% above the 1989 average. | jump to + 数字 + above … |
| homes in Tokyo remained cheaper than they were in 1989 | than + 完整从句,正好对应你正在练的比较结构 |
| The cost of an average home in Madrid rose by a further 2%. | a further 表示在上一阶段的基础上继续变化 |

**4. 和上一篇的区别**
交通事故是同一时间点的横向比较,可以用 had / recorded 描述数字。这篇是时间上的变化,要用趋势动词(fell, dropped, jumped, rose, remained stable)和 between … and … 的时间表达。

### 语法笔记

**1. rise / fall 后面用 by 还是 to?**

| 写法 | 意思 |
| --- | --- |
| rose **by** 2% | 上升了 2%(变化的幅度) |
| rose **to** 12% above the 1989 level | 上升到高于 1989 年 12% 的水平(终点) |
| rose by a further 2% | 在原来基础上再升 2% |
| ✗ fell by 7% below the 1989 level | by 和 below 混用 |
| ✗ fell to 5% | 意思变成「降到 5%」,而不是低于 1989 年 5% |
| ✓ fell to around 7% below the 1989 level | 终点写法 |
| ✓ fell by around 7% | 幅度写法(相对 1989 年) |

一句话里**二选一,不要混用**:说幅度用 by,说终点水平用 to … above / below。

**2. between 和 from**
`between 1990 and 1995`(and),或者 `from 1990 to 1995`(to)。两个词要成对,不能写 `between … to …`。

**3. price 还是 prices**
说价格的整体变化,用复数:`house prices`、`prices in Tokyo`。`price` 单数一般指某一件东西的价格。这篇你写了 7 处 price,都应该是 prices。

**4. the 1989 level**
`the 1989 level` 里的 the 不能漏;同理 `three of the five cities`。
