## 原范文

The three bar charts show average years of schooling, numbers of scientists and technicians, and research and development spending in developing and developed countries. Figures are given for 1980 and 1990.

It is clear from the charts that the figures for developed countries are much higher than those for developing nations. Also, the charts show an overall increase in participation in education and science from 1980 to 1990.

People in developing nations attended school for an average of around 3 years, with only a slight increase in years of schooling from 1980 to 1990. On the other hand, the figure for industrialize countries rose from nearly 9 years of schooling in 1980 to nearly 11 years in 1990.

From 1980 to 1990, the number of scientists and technicians in industrialize countries almost doubled to about 70 per 1000 people. Spending on research and development also saw rapid growth in these countries, reaching $350 billion in 1990. By contrast, the number of science workers in developing countries remained below 20 per 1000 people, and research spending fell from about $50 billion to only $25 billion.

## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 三图题怎么分段**
三张图,不必一图一段。范文的做法是按主题分组:
- 第 3 段:教育(图 1)。
- 第 4 段:科学(图 2 的科学家 + 图 3 的研发支出),两张图放在同一段。

**2. 概述一定要写例外**
这三张图里,**只有发展中国家的研发支出是下降的**,其余都在上升。所以概述的写法是「总体都上升,但有一个例外」。前面几篇的概述不准确,多半也是漏掉了例外。

**3. 自己算一下倍数**
`almost doubled`(几乎翻倍)用在这里不对,因为 70 ÷ 43 ≈ 1.6。写倍数和变化幅度前,先算一下:

| 实际变化 | 常用说法 |
| --- | --- |
| 约 1.6 倍 | rose by about 60% / rose from 43 to 70 |
| 约 1.8–1.99 倍 | nearly doubled |
| 约 2 倍 | doubled |
| 超过 2 倍(160 → 350 ≈ 2.2 倍) | more than doubled |
| 约一半 | fell by about half / halved |

**4. that of 和 those of / those for**
范文的概述 `the figures for developed countries … are much higher than those for developing nations` 里,figures 是复数,所以用 `those for`。如果是单数 figure,就用 `that of`。我的修改版里的 `the figure for industrialised countries … was more than three times that of developing countries` 就是单数。

**5. 时态**
有具体年份(1980、1990),用过去时。概述里也一样用过去时,但 `The charts show …` 这类描述图表本身的句子用现在时。

**6. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| the figures for X were much higher than those for Y | 两组数据整体比较 |
| with the exception of …, which fell | 概述里写例外 |
| rose from … in 1980 to … in 1990 | 描述变化 |
| with only a slight increase | with 补充程度 |
| the figure for X, at nearly 9 years, was more than three times that of Y | at … 带出数字,同时比较 |
| By contrast, … fell from about $55 billion to only $25 billion | 对比下降;only 强调少 |
| Spending on … saw rapid growth | saw 作谓语,描述增长 |

### 写作时重点盯住的几点

1. **过去时:** 1980、1990 是具体年份,全篇用过去时(was、rose、fell)。
2. **概述写例外:** 先找出有没有哪一项和整体趋势相反。
3. **倍数和幅度先算再写:** doubled、more than doubled、rose by about 60% 要和实际数字对得上。
4. **比较句:** 两边同类;复数用 those of / those for,单数用 that of;比较级不叠加(higher,不是 more higher)。
5. **统一称呼:** industrialised countries / developing countries;注意拼写 industrialised。
6. **数字逐个回图核对,每段用句号收尾,拼写对照易错词表。**
