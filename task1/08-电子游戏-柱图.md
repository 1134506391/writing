## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 动态柱图:先抓趋势和交叉点,不要逐年报数**
7 个年份 × 4 个平台 = 25 根条。不要一年一年写,而是:
- 先写整体趋势:掌机一直最高;手机和在线快速增长;主机下降。
- 再抓**交叉点**:2005 年手机和在线游戏超过主机;2006 年在线游戏又超过手机。
- 再写首尾的关键数值。

**2. 有的数据一开始就没有**
图里 2000 年只有两个平台,在线游戏 2001 年才出现,手机游戏 2002 年才出现。描述从无到有:
- `No figures are given for mobile phone or online games in that year.`
- `started to become popular`
- `first appeared in 2001`(2001 年出现了一个很小的数值)

**3. 交叉点怎么写**
`overtook` / `exceeded` / `surpassed`:`sales of mobile phone games overtook those of console games in 2005`。those of 指代 sales(复数)。

**4. 单位和数字**
图的单位是 `billions of dollars`,直接写 `$11 billion`,不用换算。和 06 一样。

**5. 时态**
2000–2006 是过去的年份,用过去时(stood at、rose、fell、reached)。`No figures are given` 描述图本身,用现在时没有问题。

**6. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| sales of handheld games stood at around $11 billion, while sales of console games were just under $6 billion | stand at 写起点;while 对比两类 |
| Over the next three years, sales … rose by about $4 billion, while those of … fell by about $1.5 billion | Over the next X years;by 写幅度;those of 避免重复 |
| Mobile phone and online games started to become popular, with sales reaching about … respectively | start to + with 补充结果 + respectively |
| In 2005, sales of … overtook those of … | 写交叉点 |
| reached peaks of nearly $18 billion, $9 billion and $7 billion respectively | respectively 一句写多个数据 |
| dropped to their lowest point, at just under $3 billion | 写最低点 |

### 语法笔记

**1. respectively 只用来对应「两个以上的主语」和「两个以上的数据」,放在句末,一句只用一次**

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| ✓ | Sales of mobile phone and online games reached about $3 billion and $2 billion **respectively**. |
| ✓ | In 2005, sales of … overtook those of console games, at about $6 billion and $5 billion **respectively**. |
| ✗ 放在动词前 | sales **respectively** overtook … |
| ✗ 一句里用好几次 | **respectively** reach to … , **respectively** close to … |
| ✗ 只有一个主语 | |

前面是 A and B,后面是数字 X and Y,A 对 X、B 对 Y,才用 respectively。

**2. sales 永远是复数**
`sales of handheld games were …`、`those of console games`。`sale`(单数)指一次买卖或促销,和「销售额」不同。对比:

| 名词 | 单复数 | 动词 | 指代 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sales | 复数 | were | those of |
| spending | 不可数 | was | that of |
| turnover | 不可数 | was | that of |

**3. reach 是及物动词**
`reached $3 billion`、`reached a peak of $18 billion`、`reaching about $3 billion`。`reach to` 不对。如果要用 to,换成 `rose to $3 billion`。

**4. 单位:先写下来,再带进每个数字**
图的单位是 `$ billions`,18 就是 `$18 billion`。你写的 `measured by $10 billion` 和 `$180 billion` 是同一个问题:把单位错读成了「10 亿」,所以 18 变成了 180。检验方法:掌机 2000 年是 $11 billion,2006 年不可能是 $180 billion,数字和前后文要对得上。读图的第一步,先在草稿纸上写下单位。

**5. 时间:over 和 for**
变化发生在一段时间里,用 `over the next three years`;`for` 用于持续的状态(`for ten years`)。

**6. 逗号不能连接两个完整分句**
`…every year, mobile phone and online games grow rapidly, …`:两个分句之间要有 `and that` 或句号。
