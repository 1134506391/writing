## 原范文

The bar chart compares the turnover in dollars from sales of video games for four different platforms, namely mobile phones, online, consoles and handheld devices, from 2000 to 2006.

It is clear that sales of games for three out of the four platforms rose each year, leading to a significant rise in total global turnover over the 7-year period. Sales figures for handheld games were at least twice as high as those for any other platform in almost every year.

In 2000, worldwide sales of handheld games stood at around $11 billion, while console games earned just under $6 billion. No figures are given for mobile or online games in that year. Over the next 3 years, sales of handheld video games rose by about $4 billion, but the figure for consoles decreased by $2 billion. Mobile phone and online games started to become popular, with sales reaching around $3 billion in 2003.

In 2006, sales of handheld, online and mobile games reached peaks of 17, 9 and 7 billion dollars respectively. By contrast, turnover from console games dropped to its lowest point, at around $2.5 billion.

## 参考修改版(Claude)

The bar chart compares global sales of four types of digital games between 2000 and 2006, measured in billions of dollars.

It is clear that handheld games had the highest sales in every year, and that sales of mobile phone and online games grew rapidly, while console sales declined. Overall, total sales more than doubled over the period.

In 2000, sales of handheld games stood at around $11 billion, while sales of console games were just under $6 billion. No figures are given for mobile phone or online games in that year. Over the next three years, sales of handheld games rose by about $4 billion, while those of console games fell by about $1.5 billion. Mobile phone and online games started to become popular, with sales reaching about $3 billion and $2 billion respectively in 2003.

In 2005, sales of both mobile phone and online games overtook those of console games, at about $6 billion and $5 billion respectively, compared with about $3 billion for consoles. In 2006, sales of handheld, online and mobile phone games reached peaks of nearly $18 billion, $9 billion and $7 billion respectively. By contrast, sales of console games dropped to their lowest point, at just under $3 billion.

## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 动态柱图:先抓趋势和交叉点,不要逐年报数**
7 个年份 × 4 个平台 = 25 根条。不要一年一年写,而是:
- 先写整体趋势:掌机一直最高;手机和在线快速增长;主机下降。
- 再抓**交叉点**:2005 年手机和在线游戏超过主机;2006 年在线游戏又超过手机。
- 再写首尾的关键数值。

**2. 有的数据一开始就没有**
图里 2000 年只有两个平台,在线游戏 2001 年才出现,手机游戏 2002 年才出现。描述从无到有:
- `No figures are given for mobile phone or online games in that year.`
- `started to become popular`
- `first appeared in 2001`(2001 年出现了一个很小的数值)

**3. 交叉点怎么写**
`overtook` / `exceeded` / `surpassed`:`sales of mobile phone games overtook those of console games in 2005`。those of 指代 sales(复数)。

**4. 单位和数字**
图的单位是 `billions of dollars`,直接写 `$11 billion`,不用换算。和 06 一样。

**5. 时态**
2000–2006 是过去的年份,用过去时(stood at、rose、fell、reached)。`No figures are given` 描述图本身,用现在时没有问题。

**6. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| sales of handheld games stood at around $11 billion, while sales of console games were just under $6 billion | stand at 写起点;while 对比两类 |
| Over the next three years, sales … rose by about $4 billion, while those of … fell by about $1.5 billion | Over the next X years;by 写幅度;those of 避免重复 |
| Mobile phone and online games started to become popular, with sales reaching about … respectively | start to + with 补充结果 + respectively |
| In 2005, sales of … overtook those of … | 写交叉点 |
| reached peaks of nearly $18 billion, $9 billion and $7 billion respectively | respectively 一句写多个数据 |
| dropped to their lowest point, at just under $3 billion | 写最低点 |

### 写作时重点盯住的几点

1. **全篇过去时。**
2. **概述:** 说清哪个平台最高、哪几个增长、哪个下降;每个结论对照图核对。
