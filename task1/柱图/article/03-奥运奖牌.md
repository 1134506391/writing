## 题目

The chart below shows the total number of Olympic medals won by twelve different countries.

## 原范文

The bar chart compares twelve countries in terms of the overall number of medals that they have won at the Olympic Games.

It is clear that the USA is by far the most successful Olympic medal winning nation. It is also noticeable that the figures for gold, silver and bronze medals won by any particular country tend to be fairly similar.

The USA has won a total of around 2,300 Olympic medals, including approximately 900 gold medals, 750 silver and 650 bronze. In second place on the all-time medals chart is the Soviet Union, with just over 1,000 medals. Again, the number of gold medals won by this country is slightly higher than the number of silver or bronze medals.

Only four other countries - the UK, France, Germany and Italy - have won more than 500 Olympic medals, all with similar proportions of each medal colour. Apart from the USA and the Soviet Union, China is the only other country with a noticeably higher proportion of gold medals (about 200) compared to silver and bronze (about 100 each).

## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 时态:这篇不是过去时**
前两篇的数据有具体年份(2002、1990–2002),所以用过去时。这篇是**历届累计总数,一直算到现在**,所以用**现在完成时**(has won、have won)和**一般现在时**(is in second place)。选时态之前,先看数据有没有限定在过去的某个时间。

**2. 怎么分组**
12 个国家不要逐个写,按总量分层:

| 层次 | 国家 | 特点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 断层领先 | USA | 约 2,300 枚 |
| 第二 | Soviet Union | 略多于 1,000 枚 |
| 第三层 | UK, France, Germany, Italy | 都超过 500 枚,金银铜比例相近 |
| 特例 | China | 金牌约 200,银铜各约 100 |
| 其余 | 其他国家 | 范文没写 |

**3. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| A total of around 2,300 medals, including approximately 900 gold, 750 silver and 650 bronze | including 后面并列,后两项省略重复的 medals |
| In second place on the all-time medals chart is the Soviet Union, with just over 1,000 medals | 倒装 + with 带出数字 |
| Again, the number of gold medals … is slightly higher than the number of silver or bronze medals | Again 衔接;**数量比数量**,than 后面重复 the number of |
| Only four other countries … have won more than 500 medals | Only … 强调数量少 |
| Apart from X and Y, Z is the only other country with … | 排除两个之后,点出唯一的特例 |

**4. 数字近似词**
`around`、`approximately`、`about`、`just over`、`more than`,按图上柱子的实际位置选用。

**5. 比较句的范本(对应你的 than 练习)**
`the number of gold medals … is slightly higher than the number of silver or bronze medals`,than 后面重复了 `the number of`,所以比较的两边都是「数量」,同类。这正是前面总结的规则:比的两边要是同类。

**6. 三篇柱图的区别**

| 篇目 | 数据特点 | 时态 | 重点 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 交通事故 | 同一年、五种交通工具 | 过去时 | 最高 / 最低、倍数比较 |
| 02 各地房价 | 两个时间段、相对基准年 | 过去时 | 趋势动词、by / to |
| 03 奥运奖牌 | 累计总数、三种奖牌 × 12 国 | 现在完成时 + 现在时 | 按总量分层、点出特例 |

### 语法笔记

**1. 数字后面的名词要用复数**
`2,300 medals`、`1,000 medals`、`more than 500 medals`、`the number of medals`、`numbers of medals`。这篇你写了约 6 处 medal 单数。图表作文里数字后面几乎总是复数名词,养成习惯:**看到数字,就检查后面的名词有没有 s。**

**2. 累计总数用现在完成时**

| 数据 | 时态 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 有具体年份(2002、1990–2002) | 过去时 | prices fell in 1995 |
| 累计到现在的总数(all-time) | 现在完成时 | The USA **has won** around 2,300 medals. |
| 当前的排名、状态 | 一般现在时 | The Soviet Union **is** in second place. |

你这篇全部用了 won,应该是 has / have won。

**3. followed by,不是 following by**
被动:`X, followed by Y`(X 排在前,Y 紧随其后)。

**4. 倍数的写法**

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| more than twice + 名词 | This is more than twice the total of the Soviet Union. |
| twice as many as | The USA has twice as many medals as the Soviet Union. |
| more than twice that of | … more than twice that of the Soviet Union. |
| ✗ twice more than | 不成立 |

**5. by far 只用于最高级**
`by far the most successful`、`by far the highest` 对。比较级用 far / much / noticeably:`far higher`、`noticeably higher`。你在 01 的版本 1 写过 `by far lower`,版本 2 改对了,这次又出现了。

**6. 逗号不能连接两个完整句子**
✗ `…, sovilet union won the second place …` → 用句号,或用 which / and。
✗ `…china is the only country, the number of gold which is …` → `China is the only country with …`。
✗ `The remaining six countries, …, they won …` → 去掉重复的主语 they。

**7. 专有名词要大写,国家名前注意 the**
the USA、the UK、the Soviet Union、China、Germany、Italy、the Olympic Games。

### 语法笔记

**1. 概述的时态**
这篇数据是累计到现在,概述写 `is by far the most successful`(现状)或 `has been`。不用 was,was 表示「过去某个时间」。

**2. 同位语:Four other countries, the UK, …, have each won**
结构是 `主语 + 逗号 + 具体名单 + 逗号 + 谓语`,名单是在解释主语,所以不能在前面加 For。`For other countries, the UK, … have won` 里,For other countries 变成了状语,主语反而不见了。

**3. 比较对象:数量对数量**
`more than twice the number of the Soviet Union` 不成立,因为 the number of the Soviet Union 是「苏联的数量」。比较的是奖牌总数,所以:
- `more than twice the total of the Soviet Union`
- `more than twice that of the Soviet Union`
- `more than twice the number of medals won by the Soviet Union`
