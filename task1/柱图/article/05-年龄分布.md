## 题目

The charts below compare the age structure of the populations of France and India in 1984.

![原图](05-年龄分布-原图.png)

### 怎么读这张图

- 每个横条是**该年龄段、该性别占全国总人口的百分比**,左边是男性,右边是女性。
- 所以把同一年龄段的男、女两条加起来,才是这个年龄段占总人口的比例。范文里的「close to 14% of people were aged 5 or under」就是印度 0–5 岁男女相加(约 7% + 6.5%)。
- 两国的年龄段划分不同:印度最高一档是 `>70`,法国则细分成 70–75、75–80、80–85、`>85`。比较老年人口时,要把法国这几档**合并**成「70 岁以上」。

## 原范文

The two charts compare the populations of France and India in terms of age distribution by gender in the year 1984.

It is clear that the population of India was younger than that of France in 1984, with a noticeably larger proportion of people aged under 20. France, on the other hand, had a significantly larger percentage of elderly inhabitants.

In India, close to 14% of people were aged 5 or under, and each five-year age bracket above this contained an increasingly smaller proportion of the population. France's population, by contrast, was more evenly distributed across the age ranges, with similar figures (around 7% to 8% of all people) for each five-year cohort between the ages of 0 and 40. Somewhere between 10% and 15% of all French people were aged 70 or older, but the equivalent figure for India was only 2%.

Looking more closely at gender, there was a noticeably higher proportion of French women than men in every cohort from age 50 upwards. For example, almost 3% of French 70- to 75-year-olds were women, while just under 2% were men. No significant gender differences can be seen on the Indian population chart.

## 这篇值得学的地方

**1. 合并年龄段再比较**
两国的年龄段划分不同,范文用 `aged 70 or older` 把法国的几档合并,才能和印度的 `>70` 比较。遇到分组不一致的图,先想怎么合并,再写。

**2. 按「年龄结构 → 性别」分段,不按国家分段**
- 第 3 段:年龄结构。印度是金字塔形,年轻人多、逐档减少;法国分布更均匀。
- 第 4 段:性别差异。法国 60 岁以上女性更多,印度没有明显差异。

两个国家放在同一段里对比,比分成「印度一段、法国一段」更清楚。

**3. 比较句的范本(对应你的 than 练习)**
`the population of India was younger than that of France`:than 后面用 `that of France`,比较的是「人口」对「人口」,同类。这就是前面总结的 that of 规则。

**4. 时态**
数据是 1984 年,用过去时(was、had、were)。对比前几篇:

| 篇目 | 数据特点 | 时态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 交通事故 / 02 各地房价 / 05 年龄分布 | 有具体年份 | 过去时 |
| 03 奥运奖牌 | 累计到现在的总数 | 现在完成时 + 现在时 |
| 04 学习动机 | 没有年份的调查数据 | 一般现在时 |

**5. 值得抄的句型**

| 句型 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| the population of India was younger than that of France | 比较两国,than that of |
| with a noticeably larger proportion of people aged under 20 | with 补充细节 |
| France, on the other hand, had a significantly larger percentage of … | 转折,换主语 |
| each five-year age bracket above this contained an increasingly smaller proportion of the population | 描述逐级递减 |
| more evenly distributed across the age ranges | 描述分布均匀 |
| the equivalent figure for India was only 2% | the equivalent figure 指对应数据 |
| from age 50 upwards | 从某个年龄往上 |
| 70- to 75-year-olds | 年龄段的名词写法 |
| No significant gender differences can be seen on … | 没有明显差异 |

**6. 词汇**
`age structure / age distribution`、`age bracket`、`cohort`、`elderly inhabitants`、`proportion / percentage`、`equivalent figure`。

### 语法笔记

**1. 1984 年 → 过去时**
你全篇用了现在时(is、has、is under 5、is more even …),约 12 处。数据是 1984 年的,用过去时:was、had、were。这一条我在上一次的要点里已经提醒过,下一版写之前,先在草稿纸上写一行「过去时」。

**2. 比较级不要叠加**

| 错误 | 正确 | 规则 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| more younger | younger | 短形容词的比较级本身带 -er,不再加 more |
| more and more small | smaller and smaller | 短形容词重复 -er |
| more elder | older / elderly | 同上;老年人用 elderly |
| more even | **more even** ✓ | 较长的形容词才用 more |
| more proportion | a larger proportion | proportion 搭配 larger / smaller / higher / lower |

01 的版本 1 里的 `more lower` 是同一类错误。

**3. population 和 people**
- 说「人口」:`the population of India`,有 the;两个国家用 `the populations of France and India`。
- 说「某个年龄的人」:`people aged under 20`、`elderly people`,不是 `popullation under 20`。
- 比例:`the proportion of the population`、`a larger proportion of people`。

**4. 比较句要完整**
`the proportion of women was noticeably higher than that of men`:要有形容词 higher,than 后面用 that of。你写的 `is far than that of man` 漏了 higher。

**5. 数字写完要核对**
这篇有两处数据手误:印度 70 岁以上写成 12%(应约 2%),法国 70–75 岁男性写成 22%(应约 2%)。有一个简单的检验:`more than 10 … while … only 12%` 前后矛盾,说明有一个数字写错了。写完逐个数字回图核对。

**6. By contrast 的拼写**
你已经三次拼错:constrat(02)、contract(02)、contact(05)。正确是 c-o-n-t-r-a-s-t。我已经把它加进易错词表。
