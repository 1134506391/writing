# 语法笔记

## 一、介词:of、in、on、at、for、with

### 先记住一个原则

英语里的介词,大部分不是靠「意思」选的,而是和前面的**动词、名词、形容词**固定搭配的。所以不要孤立地背「of 是……的」,而是把整个搭配当成一个整体记(chunk)。下面按 Task 1 里最常用的情况整理。

### 六个介词的核心用法

| 介词 | 核心含义 | Task 1 里的典型用法 | 例子 | 常见错误 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **of** | 属于 / 构成 / 数量 | the number / proportion / percentage / amount **of** + 被统计的东西;a total **of**;an increase **of** 5%(名词 + of + 幅度);most **of** the…;one **of**… | the proportion of students aged under 26 | ✗ the figure **of** industrialised countries → the figure **for** industrialised countries |
| **in** | 在……里(时间、地点、范围) | **in** 1984;**in** France;**in** the 30–39 age group;an increase **in** sales(变化的对象);**in** terms of | an increase in the number of scientists | ✗ the gap was small **in** personal stereos → small **for** |
| **on** | 关于 / 在……之上 | spend … **on**;spending **on**;the amount spent **on**;shown **on** the chart;**on** average;**on** the other hand | spending on toys | shown **in** the chart(也能说,但 on the chart 更常见) |
| **at** | 在某个点 / 数值 | **at** 40%;**at** about £145,000;stood **at**;peaked **at**;remained **at**;**at** its lowest point;**at** the age of | the figure peaked at 15% | ✗ rose **at** 12%(应为 rose to / by) |
| **for** | 对于 / 为了 / 持续 | the figure **for** India;figures **for** 1980 and 1990;**for** each of the six products;**for** ten years;the demand **for** | the figures for those in their forties | ✗ figures **from** 1980 to 1990(两个年份,应为 for 1980 and 1990) |
| **with** | 带有 / 伴随 / 具有 | , **with** only 30% being given time off;the country **with** the highest spending;compared **with**;**with** sales reaching … | with similar figures for each cohort | ✗ the same … **with** buses → the same … **as** buses |

### 判断 of / for / in 的一个窗口

写数据时,经常要在「被统计的东西」和「所属的群体 / 地点 / 年份」之间选介词:

| 结构 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| the number / proportion / amount **of** + **被统计的东西** | the number **of** scientists;the proportion **of** women;the amount **of** money |
| the figure(s) **for** + **群体 / 地点 / 年份** | the figure **for** industrialised countries;the figures **for** 1980 and 1990 |
| the number of … **in / among** + **地点 / 群体** | the number of scientists **in** industrialised countries |

一句话:**of 后面接「被数的东西」,for 后面接「数据属于谁」。**

### 变化、数字、范围

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rose / fell **by** 2% | 变化的幅度 | rose by 2% |
| rose / fell **to** 12% | 变化后的终点 | fell to 5% below the 1989 level |
| rose **from** 11 **to** 15 | 起点到终点 | rose from 43 to 70 |
| **at** 40% / stood **at** / peaked **at** / remained **at** | 停在某个数值 | stood at around $11 billion |
| fluctuated **between** 20% **and** 30% | 范围 | |
| an increase / rise / fall **of** 5% | 名词 + of + 幅度 | a rise of 5% |
| an increase / rise / fall **in** sales | 名词 + in + 变化的对象 | a fall in sales |
| an increase **in** sales **of** 5% | 两个同时出现 | an increase in sales of 5% |

## 二、连接词的位置:为什么 on the other hand / by contrast 放在主语后面?

你问的两个句子:

> France, **on the other hand**, had a significantly larger percentage of elderly inhabitants.
>
> France's population, **by contrast**, was more evenly distributed across the age ranges, …

### 它们和放在句首的写法,意思完全一样

> **On the other hand**, France had a significantly larger percentage of elderly inhabitants.
>
> **By contrast**, France's population was more evenly distributed …

两种都对。区别只在位置和效果。

### 为什么放在主语后面

1. **把新的主语放在前面,更突出。** 上一句讲印度,这一句讲法国。`France, on the other hand, had …`,读者先看到 France,马上知道话题换成了法国,连接词只是补一句「这是对比」。
2. **避免重复。** 如果每个对比句都以 `On the other hand,` / `By contrast,` / `However,` 开头,文章读起来很机械。放在主语后面可以换一种节奏。
3. **它是一个插入语,所以前后都要加逗号。** 判断方法:把中间插入的部分拿掉,句子仍然完整。
   - France, ~~on the other hand,~~ had a significantly larger percentage …(去掉后:France had …,完整)

所以并不是 `France` 「不直接跟 had」,而是 `France had` 中间插入了一个对比标记。

### 放在哪里

| 位置 | 例子 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 句首 | By contrast, Germany had the lowest overall spending. | 最常见,信号最明显 |
| **主语之后、动词之前** | Germany, **by contrast**, had the lowest overall spending. | 突出主语;前后加逗号 |
| 助动词 / be 动词之后 | Germany was, **by contrast**, the lowest spender. | 较少用 |
| 句末 | Germany had the lowest overall spending, **by contrast**. | 不推荐,Task 1 里几乎不用 |

### 注意:放在**整个主语**之后

`France's population, by contrast, was …` 里,France's population 是一个整体的主语,连接词放在整个主语之后:

- ✓ France's population, by contrast, was more evenly distributed.
- ✗ France's, by contrast, population was …

如果主语很长,放在句首更清楚。

### on the other hand 和 by contrast 的区别

- **by contrast / in contrast**:对比两个不同的事物,最适合比较数据(印度 vs 法国、英国 vs 德国)。
- **on the other hand**:从另一个角度、另一个方面看,更多用于论证(优点 / 缺点)。数据对比里也有人用,但 by contrast 更贴切。
- **however**:转折,和前面相反的情况,前后两句通常是同一个对象或同一话题。

### 同样可以移动的连接词

| 连接词 | 句首 | 主语后 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| however | However, French people spent more on … | French people, **however**, spent more on … |
| by contrast / in contrast | By contrast, spending in developing countries fell. | Spending in developing countries, **by contrast**, fell. |
| on the other hand | On the other hand, … | …, **on the other hand**, … |
| as a result / therefore | As a result, … | …, **as a result**, … / …, **therefore**, … |
| also | (不放句首) | France **also** had … |

### 练习:把句首的连接词移到主语后面

1. By contrast, Germany had the lowest overall spending.
2. However, French people spent more than Italians on photographic film.
3. By contrast, spending in developing countries fell from $55 billion to only $25 billion.

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

1. Germany, by contrast, had the lowest overall spending.
2. French people, however, spent more than Italians on photographic film.
3. Spending in developing countries, by contrast, fell from $55 billion to only $25 billion.
</details>

## 三、respectively、sales、reach

### respectively

**只用来把前面的两个(或多个)名词,和后面的两个(或多个)数据,按顺序一一对应。放在数字后面(句末),一句只用一次。**

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| ✓ | Sales of mobile phone and online games reached about $3 billion and $2 billion **respectively**. |
| ✓ | …overtook those of console games, at about $6 billion and $5 billion **respectively**. |
| ✗ 放在动词前 | sales **respectively** overtook … |
| ✗ 一句里用好几次 | **respectively** reach to … , **respectively** close to … |
| ✗ 对应不上 | 只有一个主语,或主语和数据的个数不相等 |

如果两个数据差不多,可以改用 `both`:`sales of both reached about $3 billion`。

### sales、spending、turnover

| 名词 | 单复数 | 动词 | 指代 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sales | 复数 | were | those of |
| spending | 不可数 | was | that of |
| turnover | 不可数 | was | that of |

`sale`(单数)是「一次买卖 / 促销」,和「销售额」不同。

### reach

`reach` 是及物动词,后面直接接宾语,不加 to:`reached $3 billion`、`reached a peak of $18 billion`、`reaching about $3 billion`。用 to 的是 rise:`rose to $3 billion`。

### 单位

`measured in billions of dollars`、`(in millions)`:单位用 **in**。图上写 `$ billions`,18 就是 `$18 billion`,不是 `$180 billion`。读图的第一步先写下单位。

## 四、比较级和最高级

### 规则

| 类型 | 比较级 | 最高级 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 单音节短形容词 | -er | the -est | old → older → the **oldest**;new → the **newest**;small → the **smallest**;low → the **lowest** |
| 以 -y 结尾 | -ier | the -iest | early → earlier → the **earliest** |
| 较长的形容词、分词 | more + 原形 | the most + 原形 | the **most** extensive、the **most** used |
| 「少」 | less / fewer | the least / the fewest | the **least** used、the **fewest** passengers |

**不能叠加:** ✗ more lower、more younger、the most lower、the most old、the most new、the most small。

### 速查(Task 1 常用)

| 原形 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| high | higher | the highest |
| low | lower | the lowest |
| large | larger | the largest |
| small | smaller | the smallest |
| old | older | the oldest |
| new | newer | the newest |
| young | younger | the youngest |
| long | longer | the longest |
| big | bigger | the biggest |
| fast | faster | the fastest |
| slow | slower | the slowest |
| popular | more popular | the most popular |
| extensive | more extensive | the most extensive |
| used | more used | the most used / the least used |

## 五、Although 和 but、完整的句子

- **although / though 和 but 不连用,二选一。** ✓ `Although Tokyo is third in size, it is the most used.` ✓ `Tokyo is third in size, but it is the most used.`
- **每个句子要有主语和谓语。** `located in Paris`、`having opened in 1863`、`with 394 kilometres` 都是修饰成分,不能当谓语:`The second largest system, in Paris, **is** about half the size of …`。
- **并列要平行。** ✗ `with 126 kilometres, and the annual passenger number is 144 million` ✓ `with 126 kilometres of route and an annual passenger number of 144 million`。
- **which 指代最近的名词。** 不确定时,改用 `with + 名词` 补充信息。

## 六、变化的主语、及物动词、倍数

### 1. 国家不能当变化的主语,要写数据

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| ✗ | Ireland rose too. / New York fell. |
| ✓ | **Waste production in Ireland** rose too. |
| ✓ | Ireland **saw a rise** in waste production. |
| ✓ | **Ireland's waste output** rose. |
| ✓ | **Prices in New York** fell. |

### 2. 及物动词和不及物动词

| 动词 | 用法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rise / fall | 不及物 | Waste output **rose** / **fell**. |
| increase / decrease | 两可 | Waste output **increased**. / Korea **increased** its output. |
| reduce / cut / raise | 及物(接宾语) | Korea **reduced** / **cut** its waste output. |

✗ `waste production in Korea reduce from 31 to 19`;✓ `waste production fell from 31 to 19` 或 `Korea reduced its waste output from 31 to 19`。

### 3. 倍数

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| 副词用法 | increased **more than eightfold** |
| 名词用法 | **an increase of** more than eightfold |
| ✗ | with eightfold |

0.6 → 5 是 8.3 倍,所以 `more than eightfold`;28 → 53 是 1.9 倍,所以 `almost doubled`。

### 4. 数据属于谁:for

`the figure **for** 1990`、`the totals **for** Poland and Portugal`。不写 `the figure in 1990`、`Poland and Portugal's total`。

### 5. where 后面要有完整的从句

`the only country **where** waste output fell`。✗ `where reduce total`(缺主语)。

## 七、百分比表格常用的写法

### 1. the percentage(单数)和 the percentages(复数)

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| 一个数据 | the **percentage** of owners searching the Internet rose from 41% to 73% |
| 比较两个以上的数据 | the **percentages** for text messaging and photo-taking rose steadily |
| ✗ | the percentages of calling(calling 不是被统计的对象) |
| ✓ | the percentage of owners making calls / the figure for calls |

### 2. 并列的功能要用平行的形式

✗ text message and take photo
✓ **text messaging** and **taking photos**:都是动名词(`making calls`、`playing games`、`searching the Internet`、`recording video`)。

### 3. remain at + 数值

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| ✓ | remained **at** 100%;stayed **at** the same level (41%) |
| ✗ | remain 100%;remain in 41% |

### 4. 动词后面用副词

`rose steadily`、`rose slowly`、`fell slightly`、`rose sharply`。✗ `rose steable`、`rose slow`。

### 5. 倍数

| 实际变化 | 写法 |
| --- | --- |
| 9 → 35(约 3.9 倍) | almost quadrupled / rose almost fourfold |
| 17 → 42(约 2.5 倍) | more than doubled |
| ✗ | was almost fourfold(缺动词) |

### 6. no 后面用 or

`there were no figures for Internet searches **or** video recording`,不写 `and`。

### 7. listen to music

`listen **to** music`;`use their phones to play games`。

### 8. 过去时的检查方法

「先定时态」这个提醒不够用,换成检查动作:**写完后把每个动词圈出来,逐个对着年份检查。** 只有描述表格本身的句子(`The table compares …`、`The table shows …`)用现在时,其余数据句全用过去时。

## 八、account for、share 和占比的写法

### 1. account for:主语是「占比的部分」

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| ✓ | Food, drinks and tobacco **accounted for** the largest share of consumer spending. |
| ✓ | Food accounted for 32.14% of Turkey's consumer spending. |
| ✗ | accounted the largest share(漏 for) |
| ✗ | Turkey accounted the largest share …(国家不能当主语) |
| ✓ | Turkey **had** the largest share for leisure and education. |
| ✓ | Turkey **spent** the largest proportion of its expenditure on food. |

### 2. 占比的介词:of 接总体,for 接类别,in 接国家,on 接商品

| 搭配 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| share **of** + 总体 | the largest share **of** consumer spending |
| share **for** + 类别 | the share **for** clothing and footwear |
| … **in** + 国家 | higher **in** Italy |
| spending **on** + 商品 | spending **on** food |

一个完整的句子:`Food accounted for the largest share **of** consumer spending **in** every country.`

### 3. 比例,不是金额

| 写法 | 说明 |
| --- | --- |
| ✓ the largest **proportion of** consumer spending **on** food | 比例 |
| ✓ the largest **share for** food | 比例 |
| ✗ the largest consumer spending in food | 读起来像金额 |

### 4. 其他

- `than any of the other countries`:any 和 all 不连用,of 后面要有 the。
- 每一项数据都要写清是谁的:`Spain had the smallest share for leisure and education`,不写 `leisure and education was smallest`。
- **开头句动词加 s:** `The table / chart shows / compares …`(单数主语)。写完第一句先圈出主语和动词。

## 九、poverty / poor,一类人的写法,读表对标签

### 1. poverty 是名词,poor 是形容词

| ✗ | ✓ |
| --- | --- |
| more likely poverty | more likely to be **poor** |
| classed as poverty | classed as **poor** |
| the last possible poverty | the **least likely** to be poor |
| live under poverty | live **in poverty** / live **below the poverty line** |

### 2. 一类人用复数,用表里的原词

| 表里的说法 | 正文里 |
| --- | --- |
| single aged person | single aged people |
| aged couple | aged couples |
| single, no children | single people with no children |
| couple, no children | couples without children |
| sole parent | sole parents(不是 solo parent) |
| couple with children | couples with children |

### 3. 读表:逐行对准标签

写完以后,把每个数字回到表里,和它所在的行标签核对一遍。常见的错误是把相邻的两行标签混了(Tokyo / Kyoto;19% 属于 single, no children,不属于 single aged person)。

### 4. 百分比、分数的介词

| 写法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| 百分比 + **of** + 人群 | 12% **of** couples with children |
| 分数 + **of** | just over one fifth **of** sole parents;half **of** the total |
| the figure / rate **for** + 人群 | the figure **for** couples |
| **among** + 人群 | considerably lower **among** elderly people |
| **in** poverty / **below** the poverty line | 19% … were living below the poverty line |

### 5. 数字格式

`933,000 people`;`0.933 million`(million 不加 s)。

### 6. 开头句的固定句型

**The table gives information about …** / **The table shows …** / **The table compares …**。写完第一句,先圈出主语和动词。
